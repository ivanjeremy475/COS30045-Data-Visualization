Energy Consumption in Australia

Household appliances are an important part of everyday life. Appliances such as televisions, refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners consume electricity at different rates.

Understanding appliance energy consumption can help households identify appliances that use more electricity and make more informed decisions about their energy usage.
