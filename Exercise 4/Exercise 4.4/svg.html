Exercise 4.1: SVG Shapes and Coordinate System

Demonstrating SVG primitive shapes, custom drawing features and coordinate mapping in SVG.

Steps 1, 3 & 4: House and Garden in Two Seasons

This picture uses <rect>, <circle>, <ellipse>, <line>, <polyline>, <polygon>, <path> and <text>. The two windows are placed in a <g> group.

Step 2: Changes Between Scene A and Scene B

Scene B uses the same house as Scene A, with the same body, door and window positions. The changes are in colours, a few shapes, and added or removed details that show winter instead of summer.
