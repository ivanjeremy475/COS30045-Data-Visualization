# Exercise 3: Communicating Data Insights

## Data Story
* **Target Audience:** Australian consumers planning to buy a new television.
* **Audience Characteristics:** Cost-conscious households interested in long-term electricity bill savings, non-technical background.
* **Key Questions Answered:**
  1. Does buying a higher Energy Star Rating TV actually save enough money to justify the higher upfront purchase price?
  2. How significantly does TV screen size impact annual energy consumption?
* **Visualization Guidelines:** Use clean charts with clear axes, distinct color coding for star ratings, and straightforward summary text.

## About the Data
* **Data Source:** Australian Energy Rating Database for Televisions.
* **Data Processing:** Cleaned using KNIME/Excel to handle missing values, calculate annual running costs ($AUD), and filter TV models by screen size and star ratings.
* **Privacy:** Contains only publicly available product specifications and energy testing data. No personal data is used.
* **Accuracy & Limitations:** Energy usage estimates assume standard test conditions (10 hours active, 14 hours standby per day). Actual consumption varies based on screen brightness settings, user habits, and smart features.
* **Ethics:** Visualizations utilize honest scaling (y-axes starting at zero) to prevent misleading interpretations of energy savings across TV sizes.
